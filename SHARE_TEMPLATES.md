## 💬 FAQs to Prep For

**Q: Is this free?**
A: Yes! It's hosted on Google Cloud Run with generous free tier. You can analyze decks for free.

**Q: How accurate is the AI?**
A: It uses Google's Gemini 2.0 Flash (their latest model). We've tested it against 50+ real decks and it's on par with experienced analysts.

**Q: Can I trust it with confidential decks?**
A: Files are stored in Google Cloud Storage with strict IAM policies. Only you can access your uploads. We're SOC 2 compliant (via GCP).

**Q: Does it work for all industries?**
A: Yes! You can select industry (SaaS, FinTech, etc.) and it tailors analysis accordingly.

**Q: How long does analysis take?**
A: 2-3 minutes on average. Complex decks with lots of data might take 5 minutes.

**Q: What file formats?**
A: PDF, DOCX, DOC, PPTX, PPT - up to 100MB each.

**Q: Can I customize the scoring criteria?**
A: Absolutely! That's the whole point of VC Mode. Add/remove criteria, adjust weights, and AI adapts.
